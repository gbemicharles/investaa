Forgot your transaction PIN?
If you forgot your transaction PIN, don’t worry—but it’s important to know that this PIN was created when you opened your account and it can’t be reset or recovered.

What you can do is create a new account, then move your funds to it using the internal transfer option. The good thing is that this type of transfer doesn’t require a transaction PIN, so you can move everything across in just a few minutes.

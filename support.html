Frequently Asked Questions

How long do deposits take?
Most deposits are credited within 10–30 minutes depending on blockchain network confirmation speed. All deposits go through a verification step by our team before funds are reflected in your account.
What is the minimum withdrawal amount?
The minimum withdrawal amount is $10.00 USDT. A flat $1.00 USDT processing fee is deducted from your balance on every withdrawal. Withdrawals above $100 may be subject to a security review and can take up to 24 hours to process.
How do I upgrade to a VIP rank?
Visit the VIP Club page and click "Upgrade Now" on the rank you want to reach. Upgrades are based on your cumulative direct deposit balance — funds received via internal transfers from other users do not count toward VIP eligibility. Requirements: Bronze $50, Silver $1,000, Gold $3,000, Platinum $30,000, Diamond $50,000.

Daily Investment Earnings

How do daily investment earnings work?
Every VIP member (Bronze and above) automatically earns a daily percentage return on their account balance. The return is calculated on your full current balance at the time of payout and is credited directly to your account every 24 hours — no action required on your part. You'll receive a notification and a "Daily Investment Return" entry in your transaction history each time a payout is applied.
What are the daily earning rates for each VIP tier?
Daily returns are paid based on your VIP rank:

🥉 Bronze — 1% per day
🥈 Silver — 2% per day
🥇 Gold — 2.5% per day
💎 Platinum — 4% per day
👑 Diamond — 5% per day

Regular (non-VIP) accounts do not earn daily returns. Upgrade your account to start earning.
Do my earnings compound over time?
Yes — your earnings are fully compounding. Each payout is added to your balance before the next cycle is calculated, so your returns grow automatically over time. For example, a $1,000 balance at Gold tier earns $25 on day one, then $25.63 on day two, and keeps growing from there without you needing to do anything.
When will I receive my first daily earning?
Your first earning is credited 24 hours after you become eligible (i.e., after you hold a VIP rank with a positive balance). After that, payouts continue every 24 hours automatically. If the server restarts, it picks up right where it left off — you will never be skipped or double-paid.
Why haven't I received my daily earnings yet?
There are a few reasons this might happen: (1) Your account is still on the Regular rank — only Bronze and above are eligible. (2) Your balance is $0.00 — you need a positive balance to earn. (3) It has been less than 24 hours since your last earning or since you upgraded. If none of these apply and you still haven't received your earning, please contact our support team.
Do transferred funds earn daily returns?
Yes — once funds are in your account (from any source), they contribute to your earning balance as long as you hold a VIP rank. The distinction only matters for VIP upgrades: only direct deposits count toward your upgrade requirement, not funds transferred to you by other users. But for earning purposes, your full account balance is used.

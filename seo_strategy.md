# SEO Strategy

## In scope
- Public authentication entry pages: `/login.html`, `/register.html`, `/forgot-password.html`, `/verify.html`
- Root crawl-governance files: `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/llms.txt`, and favicon assets
- Server routing and auth behavior that changes what crawlers receive on public or protected routes

## Out of scope
- Authenticated dashboard workflows after sign-in
- Admin workflows after sign-in
- Internal account utilities, except where they are publicly crawlable or indexable because of routing/auth implementation

## Target audience
- Prospective and returning account holders looking for branded login, sign-up, verification, and support access

## Primary keywords
- Unknown
- Likely branded navigational queries such as "InvestAA login", "InvestAA register", and "InvestAA support"
